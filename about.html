About Five Stone

Serving the body of Christ and the community through the love of God.

Our church

At Five Stone, we believe everyone should have the opportunity to receive the love of God and the gift of salvation through His Son, Jesus Christ. We strive to express the love of God in everything we do as we serve the body of Christ and the community in which we live.

Our Mission

To grow each day in Christ, trust Him, and make Him known to the world by sharing the Word of God.

Our Vision

We honor the Word of God as truth and embrace His love, sharing it with all mankind as an expression of our love for Him—according to John 3:16.

Our pastor

A heart for God and people

Pastor Battle has a passion for studying, teaching, and preaching the Word of God. He is a family man who values time with his loved ones and the memories they create together.

Friendly and encouraging, Pastor Battle never meets a stranger. He takes his calling seriously and seeks to glorify God as a husband, father, brother, uncle, and pastor.
